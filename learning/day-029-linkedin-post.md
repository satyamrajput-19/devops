🚀 **Day 29 — Kubernetes Basics | DevOps & Cloud Engineering**

Today I practised the Kubernetes building blocks used to manage application workloads beyond a single Pod.

🔹 Deployments and ReplicaSets  
🔹 Labels and selectors  
🔹 Scaling, rolling updates, rollout history and rollback  
🔹 ConfigMaps for non-sensitive configuration  
🔹 Secrets and safe credential-handling practices  
🔹 Readiness and liveness probes  
🔹 Namespaces and ResourceQuotas  
🔹 Blue/green deployment using a Service selector

**Key takeaway:** Deployments manage desired application state, ReplicaSets maintain Pod replicas, and Services route traffic to matching endpoints. Readiness and liveness probes serve different purposes, and Kubernetes Secrets still require proper access control and encryption.
