🚀 Day 21 of my DevOps & Cloud Engineering journey

Today I explored Jenkins CI/CD and how pipelines automate software delivery.

🔹 Jenkins controller, agents, and executors
🔹 Declarative Jenkinsfile syntax and pipeline stages
🔹 Build triggers: webhooks, SCM polling, and schedules
🔹 Plugins for Git, Docker, and Maven
🔹 Artifact versioning and build traceability
🔹 Integrating container image scanning with Trivy
🔹 Jenkins credentials, least privilege, and pipeline security

Key takeaway: a reliable CI/CD pipeline is more than a sequence of commands. It needs repeatable stages, traceable artifacts, secure credentials, and clear failure behavior when required checks do not pass.
